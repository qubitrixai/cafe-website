One espresso machine and a lot of stubbornness.

Amber & Oak began as a tiny coffee cart outside the university gates. Today it's a warm little café, but the idea hasn't changed.

From a cart to Maple Street

In 2016, our founder Maya was a student who couldn't find a quiet place nearby with good coffee and food that didn't cost a fortune. So she bought a second-hand espresso machine, built a cart, and started serving lattes to classmates.

The queue kept growing. Nearby office workers started showing up, then families on weekends. In 2019 we moved into an old bookshop on Maple Street, kept the wooden shelves, and turned it into the café you see today.

What we believe in

We'd rather do a few things really well than a hundred things okay. These are the promises we try to keep every day.

  1. Fresh, every day

    Bread, pastries and soups are made in our kitchen each morning. Nothing reheated from a factory.

  2. Fair and local

    Our beans come from fair-trade farms. Our milk, eggs and veg come from local suppliers.

  3. Honest prices

    Good food shouldn't be a luxury. Students get 10% off with a valid ID, all day.

  4. Room for everyone

    Quiet corners for studying, big tables for families, and a kids' menu that isn't just chips.
